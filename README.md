# Company Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing company sales performance through an interactive dashboard developed using Microsoft Excel as part of an assignment by The MindShift Movement.

The dashboard provides insights into revenue trends, product contribution, market share, and the relationship between revenue and units sold to support business performance evaluation and decision-making.

## Project Objectives

- Evaluate monthly revenue trends and identify periods of strong and weak sales performance.
- Analyse product contributions to total revenue.
- Examine market share across business categories.
- Compare revenue against units sold to understand sales performance.

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Monthly Revenue Trends
Reveals periods of strong and weak sales performance, helping management identify seasonal patterns and opportunities for improvement.

### 2. Product Contribution Analysis
Highlights products generating the highest revenue and those that may require improved marketing, pricing adjustments, or inventory review.

### 3. Market Share Analysis
Identifies the business's strongest categories to support investment prioritisation.

### 4. Revenue vs. Units Sold
Provides a comparison of revenue and sales volume, highlighting that higher sales volume does not necessarily translate into higher revenue.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence

## Project Outcome
The project demonstrates how sales data can be transformed into visual insights that help businesses understand performance, identify opportunities, and make informed strategic decisions.
